Deeds, not words

The Sphere proves the Third Dimension by what he does: he takes a tablet out of a locked cupboard without opening it, and touches the Square inside his stomach. Each scene shows the room from above, as we see it, and Spaceland from the side, where the whole of Flatland is one line.
