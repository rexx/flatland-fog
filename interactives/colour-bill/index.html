The Colour Bill

The Bill would paint every woman and every Priest red in front and green behind. Seen from the right angle, the two would then look the same. The preface to the second edition says the Square identified himself “perhaps too closely” with Flatland’s historians, in whose pages the destinies of women “have seldom been deemed worthy of mention and never of careful consideration.”
